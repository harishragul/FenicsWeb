De Vahl Davis Cavity Benchmark

← Back to Validation Dashboard

The De Vahl Davis (1983) differentially-heated square cavity is a classic natural-convection benchmark: a hot left wall (u=1), a cold right wall (u=0), and insulated (zero-flux) top and bottom walls. FenicsWeb solves the steady thermal diffusion equation only, i.e. the Ra → 0 (pure conduction) limit of the full natural-convection problem. In this limit the exact solution is u(x,y) = 1 − x (independent of y), giving an exact average Nusselt number of Nu = 1.0 along the hot wall — a useful target for verifying the Nusselt-number post-processing itself.
